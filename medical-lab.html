📖 How to Read Results

1. Identify the test. Understand what the test measures.

2. Check the units. A number without its unit may be misleading.

3. Review the laboratory reference interval. Use the range printed on the actual report.

4. Consider the clinical context. Symptoms, medications, age, and other tests may affect interpretation.

5. Escalate concerning findings. Follow local clinical protocols and inform the responsible clinician.

لا يُشخّص المرض اعتماداً على تحليل واحد فقط. يجب ربط النتائج بالأعراض والفحص السريري وبقية المعلومات الطبية.
